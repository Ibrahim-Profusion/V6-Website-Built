01 · Why we exist

A homeowner with a high bill shouldn't need an MBA to fix it.

The math is doable. The rebate stack is real. The right installer is out there. It just isn't a homeowner's job to assemble it.

We built the platform a homeowner would build for themselves, if they had a year to do it. One report. One ranked plan. One promise that the math is honest and the recommendation is the math, not a pitch.

Our mission

We're not paid by any single contractor or manufacturer. The recommendation is just the math.

02 · How we work

Engineers compute. Handyman explains. That's the whole company.

A real model under the hood. A four-page report on top. Three principles hold the platform together.

01
A real underlying score, expressed as a letter grade.

An engineering model weighs envelope, mechanicals, generation, climate zone, and utility mix. You see a letter and a ranked upgrade plan.

02
A rebate stack synced weekly, not quoted from memory.

Federal, state, and utility incentives pulled into a current database. When a cap changes or a tier opens, the report changes the same week.

03
Six installer gates, re-earned after every job.

License, insurance, time in market, public reviews, manufacturer certification, written workmanship warranty. Fewer than 1 in 4 applicants make it through.
