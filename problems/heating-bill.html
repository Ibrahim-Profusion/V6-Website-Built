Context

Why your bill keeps climbing

Heating oil runs $3.40-$4.20 per gallon in winter. Natural gas rates climbed again in 2025-2026. Electric resistance heat is the worst of all, at around $0.28/kWh delivered, a single-room electric heater can cost $3-5 per day. A well-tuned heat pump running on the same grid delivers the same heat for a quarter of the cost. The technology exists now. The rebates to install it are at historic highs.
