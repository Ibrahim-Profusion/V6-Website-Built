New Jersey

The Garden State's whole-house approach.

New Jersey runs one of the most coherent whole-home incentive programs in the country. NJ Clean Energy's Whole House Pilot pays up to $6,000 cash plus $25,000 in zero-interest financing to upgrade the entire envelope and systems together. SuSI / SREC-II pays solar owners $85.90/MWh for 15 years.

A real example · Newark, NJ

What a gas-to-heat-pump rebate stack actually looks like.

A 3-ton ducted heat pump qualifies for the NJ Clean Energy whole-home rebate at $4,000, layered with NJ Comfort Partners income-qualified weatherization (up to $5,000) and the NJ SuSi solar program (TREC payments).
