Maryland

The Old Line State's fossil-to-electric push.

Maryland's EmPOWER program (administered through BGE, Pepco, Delmarva, SMECO, and Potomac Edison) pays up to $15,000 to switch from fossil heating to a heat pump system. Maryland Solar Access pays $750/kW up to $7,500 for solar, one of the few states with both an active solar rebate and a state energy storage program.

A real example · Baltimore, MD

What a gas-to-heat-pump rebate stack actually looks like.

A 3-ton ducted heat pump qualifies for the EmPOWER Maryland rebate at $3,000, layered with the EmPOWER insulation tier (up to $2,500) and the new MD Solar Access Program. EmPOWER 0% APR financing covers up to 10 years.
