A real example · Natick, MA

What a gas-to-heat-pump rebate stack actually looks like.

A 3-ton ducted heat pump (R-32, cold-climate certified) qualifies for the MassSave whole-home rebate at $2,650 per ton: $7,950 stacked toward your project. The Manual J sizing bonus adds $500 when the installer sizes to 90-120% of design heat load, and the new New England Heat Pump Accelerator contributes $650 more in 2026. The 0% APR HEAT Loan finances the install over seven years, so most MassSave projects move forward with no money down on day one.
