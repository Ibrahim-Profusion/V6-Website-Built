The Home Efficiency Score

One number. Everything that actually matters.

Your Score pulls together your house, your utility, and the moment you're in — and turns all of it into a single number that ranks every upgrade you could make. Higher Score, lower bills, fewer surprises.

  1. Your home, read deeply.
    How it's built, how tight it runs, what it's burning today.

    Your rate today, the trajectory it's on, the fine print most homeowners never see.

    What's on top, what hits it, what the sun can earn for you.

    Every program funding right now — not last year's brochure.

The Method

Three steps. No pitch. No pressure.

We give you the data. If you decide to move forward, we introduce you to one vetted local partner — never five, never a call center, never a number you'll get for months.

homepage-step-1-score
i.
We score your home

A short questionnaire combined with public records — permits, assessor data, climate zone, utility rates — generates your 400–800 score, grade, and a prioritized upgrade list.

← About sixty seconds

ii.
We stack your rebates

Your report cross-references every program you qualify for — MassSave, Clean Heat RI, Efficiency Maine, NHSaves, Efficiency Vermont, Energize CT — with April 2026 data. Average homeowner qualifies for $14K+ they've never claimed.

← Delivered as a PDF

iii.
You decide what's next

If you want to move forward, we introduce you to one pre-qualified installer with 5+ years' experience, A+ BBB, verified insurance. If you just want the data — that's fine too.

What we put in writing

Two guarantees. No fine print.

Most matching services stop caring the moment they hand off your information. These two commitments go with every installer introduction we make.

Guarantee 01

We stand with you.

If something's off, you call us. We call them. You never chase your installer alone. Every dispute, every delay, every detail you're not happy with — we step in on your side until it's resolved.

Guarantee 02

We don't guess who we send.

The best installer for your home — not the nearest one. Every installer in our network earns their spot and keeps it. We score them after every job and re-weigh the match against your home, your upgrade, and your zip code.
