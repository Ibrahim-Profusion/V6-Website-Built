## Photography style guidelines

- **Real over stock.** Every image should feel like an actual ProFusion project or a recognizable New England / Mid-Atlantic / Florida home. Stock photography reads as inauthentic and dilutes the brand voice.
- **Warm, natural light.** Golden hour, soft window light, no harsh fluorescent or studio strobes. The brand's restraint extends to the photography.
- **People in service moments, not glamour shots.** Installer with a tape measure, homeowner reading a report, tech tuning a heat pump. Show the work, not the marketing.
- **Diagnostic, not promotional.** Photograph what the work actually looks like — including imperfect angles, real homes with real wear, a thermal camera showing real heat loss. The aesthetic is honest, not aspirational.
- **Color palette consistency.** Lean into ProFusion blue (`#1A4F90`) and ProFusion green (`#3AB44A`) where they appear naturally (uniforms, equipment, branded vehicles) but don't force them.
- **No faces of named real homeowners without explicit consent.** For testimonial slots, the named individuals (e.g., Elizabeth E.) need signed photo releases.

## How to swap placeholders for real images

1. Save your image in `/images/` with the slot ID as filename (e.g. `images/state-hero-massachusetts.jpg`)
2. Open the relevant HTML file
3. Find the `<div class="img-placeholder" data-slot="...">...</div>` block
4. Replace it with: `<img src="images/your-filename.jpg" alt="Descriptive alt text">` (root pages) or `<img src="../images/your-filename.jpg" alt="...">` (subfolder pages)
5. CSS aspect ratios and border-radius are handled by the wrapper class — no extra styling needed
