A small team doing hard, useful work.

The work is technical (real tariff data, real performance modeling, real install vetting) but the writing on the page in front of a homeowner is plain English. 01 · Why work here

The problem is interesting. The customer is real.

Most companies in this category sell. We score. The shift from a sales-led to a math-led product means engineers, writers, and analysts all do the actual thinking. Nobody on the team is a closer.

Picture a Wirecutter team that also ships the recommendation engine. We compute against a real underlying model. We sync utility tariffs and rebate programs weekly. We audit every installer on six gates per job and re-audit after every install. Then we hand a homeowner a four-page PDF that reads like a friend who happens to have looked at their house. The whole company is the bridge between those two surfaces.

The market is also genuinely large. Residential energy is a $230 billion annual category in the US, and the homeowner side of it is fragmented into one Federal credit, fifty state programs, and several thousand utility rebates that change every quarter. Whoever assembles that mess into a single answer keyed to a specific address has a real product. We're that company.

What we don't do

We don't ship features for the sake of shipping. We don't add a chatbot to look modern. We don't run paid ads on Black Friday. The team that wants to do plain, hard work and let it compound is the team we're building.

02 · What we look for

Three traits, weighted heavily.

Resumes get a glance. These three things get the read.

i.
A bias for plain English.

If you can take a thermal-envelope calculation, a federal tax-credit interaction, and a utility tariff schedule, then explain the whole thing in two paragraphs that a homeowner reads at the kitchen table, you'll fit. The hardest part of the job isn't the math; it's the translation. Look for people who can do both.

ii.
Patience with detail.

Rebate amounts change every quarter. Refrigerants get delisted. Utility tariff filings come out with three weeks of notice. Our work compounds in small honest increments, not big rebuilds. We hire people who'd rather be 1% better every week for a year than 50% better one quarter then revert.

iii.
Skin in the homeowner's game.

Most of our team has either owned a home, walked through someone else's renovation, or watched a parent get sold a $44,000 solar lease that didn't pencil. You don't have to have personally lived it. But you do need to be able to picture the homeowner reading your work in the evening, on their phone, with a heating bill they can't explain.

04 · How we treat people

The benefits that actually mattered to us when we joined.

Standard things, done well. We listed the ones we use ourselves.

Health, dental, vision · from day one
No 90-day waiting period. Premium fully covered for the employee, 50% for dependents. PPO and HDHP options.
Flexible PTO · minimum 20 days
No accrual cap, no carryover penalty. Minimum 20 days a year used (we mean it, the leadership team checks).
Remote-friendly, two offices anchored
Most of the team works remote. Boston (Charlestown) and Hicksville offices are open for anyone who wants to come in. Quarterly all-hands rotates between them.
401(k) with 4% match
Vests immediately. We auto-enroll new hires at 6% pre-tax with the option to change anytime.
A free Home Efficiency Score for your house
Plus our installer-rate pricing if you decide to upgrade. We dogfood the product on our own homes; nothing teaches the work like running it on your own envelope.
$1,500/year learning stipend
Books, conferences, certifications, anything that makes you better at the work. We approve almost everything. The bar is "useful to the homeowner you'll be writing for."
05 · How to reach us

A short note beats a polished resume.

We've hired people from the carpentry trades, from utility-policy analyst roles, from Stripe, from Vermont weatherization crews, and from journalism. What they share isn't the resume; it's the work.

If you'd like to be considered for a future opening, send us a short note. Tell us what kind of work you'd want to do at ProFusion, and link to one or two things you've made (a piece of writing, a side project, a calculator you built for yourself, a documentation page you wrote because nobody else would). We read every note. We respond to most within two weeks.

Use the contact page with the subject line Careers, or email careers@profusion.solutions directly.
