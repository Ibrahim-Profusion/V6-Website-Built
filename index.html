The Method

Three steps. No pitch. No pressure.

We read your home. We do the math. We hand you a vetted installer when you're ready. You make one phone call. We do the rest.

01
About 5 minutes

We build your home's profile.

Drop your address. We pull what's already public, square footage, age, roof orientation, climate zone. You add a few things only you know: the rooms that get cold, the bills that hurt. A few clicks, that's the only legwork you do.

02
We do the homework

We figure out what's best to do next.

We pull satellite imagery, your local electricity and heating rates, and every rebate funded in your zip code. We cross-reference all of it against what you told us. You get a four-page report ranking the upgrades, with the rebates already in the math.

03
We hand you the installer

We match you with an installer worth trusting.

When you're ready, we hand-pick an installer for your job. Every name on our list cleared six checks before they got there, real license, real insurance, real customer reviews, real workmanship warranty. We stay on the line until the work is done and the rebate has landed. If something's off, you call us. We call them.

The Home Efficiency Score

One number. Everything that actually matters.

Your Score pulls together your house, your utility, and the moment you're in, then turns all of it into a single letter grade ranking every upgrade you could make.

  1. Your home, read deeply.
    How it's built, how tight it runs, what it's burning today.
  2. Your utility, read honestly.
    Your rate today, the trajectory it's on, the fine print most homeowners never see.
  3. Your roof and your sun.
    What's on top, what hits it, what the sun can earn for you.
  4. The live rebate stack.
    Every program funding right now, not last year's brochure.
  5. The moment you're in.
    Rate-hike windows, credit cliffs, the things expiring while you wait.

And several more inputs we don't put on a brochure. The math is the moat.
