06 · Questions Homeowners Ask

The honest FAQ.

Actual questions that come up in the first installer conversation, answered for a typical New Hampshire homeowner in 2026.

Does my municipal utility qualify?

No, only customers of Eversource, Liberty, Unitil, or NH Electric Co-op. Municipal utility customers should check with their local utility directly.
