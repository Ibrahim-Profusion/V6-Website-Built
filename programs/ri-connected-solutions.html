Electric bills have climbed every year.

Rhode Island residential electricity climbed from roughly 14.4¢/kWh in 2010 to 31.3¢/kWh today, averaging 5% per year. A battery sidesteps that climb on two fronts: peak-shaving evening kWh, and locking in $225/kW ConnectedSolutions payments for the next five summers.

04 · Honest FAQ

The honest FAQ.

Actual questions that come up in the first installer conversation, answered for a typical Rhode Island homeowner in 2026.

What if my battery dispatches during an outage?

It doesn't. ConnectedSolutions only dispatches during grid peak events, not outages. Your battery prioritizes your home backup during any grid interruption.

How much can I really earn?

Depends on how much capacity you provide during events. A 13.5 kWh Powerwall dispatching at full capacity typically earns $750-$1,200/year. Opt-outs of individual events reduce earnings proportionally.
