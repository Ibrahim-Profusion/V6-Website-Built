Your rate climbed 35% in 15 years.

Florida's residential electric rate climbed from 12¢/kWh in 2010 to 15.8¢/kWh by 2025. Florida pairs two stacked tax exemptions with year-round production: a 100% property tax exemption on the system's added value, plus a 6.5% sales tax waiver on equipment.

A real example · Tampa, FL

What an 8 kW solar install in Florida actually pays back.

An 8 kW rooftop array on a Tampa home generates ~13,000 kWh/yr. Florida's 100% solar property tax exemption removes the added home value from your assessment, and the 6.5% sales tax exemption stays in your pocket on day one. Net metering at retail rate locks in for 25 years.

From first call to permission to operate.

A typical Florida residential solar install runs 7–11 weeks from site survey to grid interconnection. Permitting and inspection sit on most of that runway.

01.
Site survey & system design
Contractor evaluates roof orientation, shade, structural load, and produces a system design with an annual production estimate.

02.
Permitting & utility interconnection app
Building permits filed with the town; interconnection application filed with the local utility. Review typically runs 6–8 weeks.
Weeks 2-4

Equipment procurement
Panels, inverter, racking, and balance-of-system components ordered after permit approval. Lead times depend on configuration.
Weeks 3-6
04.
Installation
Most residential installs complete in 1–3 days on-site. Building permit inspection follows a few days later.
Weeks 6-8

Final utility inspection and net-metering activation. PTO is when the system legally begins exporting and earning credits.

06 · Questions Homeowners Ask

The honest FAQ.

Actual questions that come up in the first installer conversation.

Is there really no FL state rebate?

Correct, no statewide cash rebate. The only FL solar incentives are the two tax exemptions plus net metering.

Should I lease or buy in FL now?

Worth modeling both. Lease/PPA carries the commercial Section 48 ITC through as lower lease payments.
